## Likely judge questions

**How do you handle mistakes?** Exact source spans verify evidence. Deterministic checks flag unresolved fields and contradictory values. Reviewers record corrections and the next approved revision.

**How much does it cost to run?** The demo uses local Ollama, SQLite and open-source dependencies. It needs no paid inference service, hosting, API key or subscription.

**What needs host input next?** Confirm the sandbox field mapping, approved price tables, law firm precedents, calculation rules and accessibility guidance. The demo marks its fictional substitutes.

**Can every workflow be configured?** Standard fields, conditional rules, reviewer roles and output formats are configurable. Specialised calculation and document adapters handle the more complex cards.

**What does the model do on an ambiguous time?** It captures the literal wording. The validator leaves the normalized time unresolved until someone confirms it.
